Reflection

My automotive work has built my end-to-end production skills, from filming through to post. I tailor content for both cinematic film work and social platforms like TikTok and Instagram. My short-form pieces suit social, while my university final project pushed me towards commercial and film-oriented work.

I shoot on the Panasonic LUMIX S1H alongside the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 and 4, which lets me work at both a high-end cinematic level and a fast, lightweight one. Improving my videography alongside my post-production experience has taught me how the decisions I make on camera affect editing and final delivery.

A major factor in this growth was moving my post-production workflow into DaVinci Resolve, particularly for colour. I spent most of my editing hours in After Effects and Premiere, so learning Resolve and node-based grading was a steep but valuable curve. It has made me far more aware of colour as a storytelling and branding tool.

My final project explored filming cars from both commercial and documentary angles, making polished content while experimenting with narrative filmmaking. It is still a work in progress, and I'll keep adding to this page as I make more.
